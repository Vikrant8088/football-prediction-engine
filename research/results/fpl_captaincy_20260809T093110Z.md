## Read

`att` — never captaining a goalkeeper or defender — is the concrete, zero-downside win: it aligns with universal FPL practice and only ever *helps*, since a GK/DEF almost never out-hauls the best attacker in your XI. The `ceiling` rules test whether, AMONG attackers, chasing variance (the Haaland case) beats the mean. If they don't clear the bar, captaining the highest-ceiling attacker is a RANK/differential play, not a raw-points one.
