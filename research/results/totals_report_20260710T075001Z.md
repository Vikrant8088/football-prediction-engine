## Verdict

**No model turns a statistically significant profit at any price level.** Even where the engine's probability is competitive, the bookmaker's margin is a toll that swallows the difference. The goals market is closed too.

## Caveats

- Betting the best closing price across books is optimistic: those prices are not always reachable, and accounts that win get limited.
- Overround removed by proportional normalisation.
- EPL only; the sharpest league. Odds are a yardstick, never a model input.
